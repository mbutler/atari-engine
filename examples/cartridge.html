ATARI ENGINE / REFERENCE CARTRIDGE

Catch.

A deliberately dull game. It exists to show how a cartridge wires to the engine and to hold itself to 1982 scope: one verb, one screen, four variations, and every byte of its state in the 128 the console would have had. Read cartridge.mjs beside it.
