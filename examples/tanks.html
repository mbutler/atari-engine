ATARI ENGINE / TWO PLAYERS

Tanks.

Two joysticks, two tanks. Turn, drive, and fire — first to five hits wins. Play with two gamepads or share a keyboard.

PAUSED

Left / blueW / S drive forward / reverse · A / D turn · Space fires or starts
Right / red↑ / ↓ drive forward / reverse · ← / → turn · Enter fires or starts
GamepadsPad 1 is blue; pad 2 is red. Stick or D-pad turns and drives, either main face button fires. Press a button to activate each pad.
FiringHold fire to reload and shoot again. One shell per tank; simultaneous hits count for both players.
Variation1 selects open field or cover before a match
Console2 or R resets · 3 toggles colour · 4 / 5 give blue / red a longer reload
PauseP pauses or resumes. Leaving the window pauses both players.
